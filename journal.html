June 30, 2026

Finishing Act 1 of STBF, and Why I Needed To

STBF exists because I needed to answer a question the other two stories couldn't: can I actually finish something? EPMC runs on a light-novel schedule with no end in sight, and SBM is an ongoing web novel by design. So I wrote STBF short and focused on purpose — four acts, one volume, meant to close. Act 1 is done. Act 2 is on hold while I get further into EPMC Vol. 3, which is already deep into its first draft. Taking a break after finishing a chunk of work is basically tradition for me at this point.

Early 2026

Why SBM Exists

EPMC was never built to hold every idea I had, so this became the outlet — something simpler, and honestly more relaxing to write. Publishing into a quiet room with zero comments and zero engagement is hard, and I mean that literally. But one reader followed early on, and that alone was enough to make the whole thing feel worth continuing. The plan is somewhere around 50 chapters, subject to change, and I don't intend to leave it unfinished — even if it takes a while.

SBM
2025 – 2026

The Afterword I Never Rewrote

EPMC started after a Mushoku Tensei watch session, when a friend flinched at how easily the protagonist was accepted as family despite talent standing in for love. That stuck with me, and it's the seed the whole story grew from — someone ordinary and scarred who still finds a way back to love, written as a kind of tribute after Re:Zero hit every emotional beat I hoped to someday earn myself. I know the early chapters run long and over-explain themselves. I'm working on that. A Kindle edition is still the plan, whenever time allows.
