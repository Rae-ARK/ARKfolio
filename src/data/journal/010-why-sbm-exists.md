title: Why SBM Exists
---
EPMC was never built to hold every idea I had, so this became the outlet — something simpler, and honestly more relaxing to write. Publishing into a quiet room with zero comments and zero engagement is hard, and I mean that literally. But one reader followed early on, and that alone was enough to make the whole thing feel worth continuing. The plan is somewhere around 50 chapters, subject to change, and I don't intend to leave it unfinished — even if it takes a while.
