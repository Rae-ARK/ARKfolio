I found all three stories copied and hosted in full on Novels2.com without my permission. I don't mind sites that index my work and send readers to the original — that helps people find stories. Hosting a complete copy is something else: it's unauthorised redistribution. If you'd like to support the work, please read it on Royal Road, Scribble Hub, or through Amazon KDP and the stores connected to it, and let other readers and writers know mirror sites like this exist. If you've been through a DMCA or copyright process before, I'd be grateful to hear how it went.
