Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This site and app (“Rae ARK”) is an author portfolio. It exists to showcase web novels, journal updates, and ways to read or support the work. This policy explains what little data is involved and why.

What's collected

The site itself does not run analytics, trackers, or advertising, and it does not create accounts or store personal data on a server. The only place personal information can enter the picture is the Feedback page, which opens your own email app with a pre-filled address and subject — anything you write there is sent directly from your device using your own email provider, under that provider's privacy policy, not ours.

App permissions (Android)

The Android app requests internet access only. This is used to open external links — Royal Road, Scribble Hub, X/Twitter, and similar — in your browser, and to check for updated content on future visits. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other device permission.

Local storage

A single preference — whether you've chosen light or dark mode — is saved on your own device so it's remembered next time. It never leaves your device and isn't linked to you personally.

Third-party links

Buttons and links to Royal Road, Scribble Hub, X/Twitter, and retail storefronts take you to third-party sites with their own privacy policies. Once you leave this site or app, this policy no longer applies.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and no data is knowingly collected from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Feedback page, or to contact@rae-ark.example.
