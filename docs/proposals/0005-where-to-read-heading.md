# 0005 — Update the "Where to Read" heading

- **Status:** Implemented — see [changelog](../implementation/changelog.md)

## Problem

The home page's "Where to Read" heading said "Every chapter, free, wherever
you already read". Since [0002](0002-paperback-card-on-home.md) added a paid
paperback card to that section, "free" was no longer true of everything under
it.

## Change

New heading: **"Read every chapter free, or keep a copy on your shelf"**. It
keeps the free-to-read promise and names the print option, echoing the
footer quote about books sitting on shelves.
