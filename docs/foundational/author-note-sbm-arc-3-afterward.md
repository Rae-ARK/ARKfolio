Hello people. It's me, Rae ARK. It's been quite a while since my health has failed me for a few days, following that life suddenly took an unexpected turn as I got busy with others… rather unavoidable matters.

To be honest it's a tradition. To take a few days break between arcs. Yes, I know what you're probably thinking. "This guy just wrote one chapter and is already thinking about taking another break?" I hear folks. But this time it's rather short. Arc 4 starts next week. Arc 3 was something I wanted to explore. Really. But, as an author, I still have a long way to go.

Whenever I take breaks, I go back and read the chapters I've written. Sometimes it's good. Most of the time, I'll be like. Yeah, that one sucked. Then. How can I improve it? Learn from it?

All sorts of questions pop up. Yeah it's a mess. Enough yapping about my sob story though. Let's bring the focus back on SBM.

SBM arc 3 - duty in their absence, introduced so many cool things. Yeah I know, I believe I have improved as a writer and some stuff was quite decent, even if that's my own opinion.

Chapters like 21, 19 and others were such a blast to write, I had an enjoyable experience writing it.

As a reader? What are your opinions on arc 3 - duty in their absence? What about SBM as a whole so far? I'd love to know, if interested, share in the comments. I always make some time to read every one of them.

I don't really have much else to share this time. So bye folks. I'll see you all next week.

--- Rae ARK
