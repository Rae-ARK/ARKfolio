Hello everyone this is me. Rae ARK. The author behind this fictions you've read. The Shadow I Cast Over Two Beautiful Flowers (STBF).

Above everything, let me first thank you all who read it. Every view helps and I appreciate every single one of you.

The concept for this fiction is something I always wanted to write about, because it's literally my kind of requirement. If any of you are familiar with my other fictions Enigmatic Pathways Mystic Circuits (EPMC) and Summoned by Mistake, I Decided to Learn How to Live (SBM). Then I'm sure the similarities could be seen through the seams.

Let's talk about this fiction, the idea behind this fiction has an unusual origin. I won't bore you all with too much detail, but allow me to share a concise version.

For those who are unfamiliar with EPMC, I started writing it because I was motivated after I read and experienced anime media, notable among them. Mushoku Tensei and Re Zero. Both of them left a deep impression on me and was the foundation for me to start my journey to starting my own stories other people could experience.

The specific motivation behind STBF was so simple some might just laugh at the reason. EPMC was a very big story and it's not reaching its conclusion anytime soon as it follows a LN release schedule. SBM was written as a companion to EPMC, but it follows a WN release schedule.

So I was stuck with a problem. Am I capable of finishing narratives with satisfying conclusions? The answer… I don't know. So I wrote several concepts/ideas of multiple fictions which I want to keep them short and incredibly focused so I have to arrive at the conclusion sooner than EPMC and SBM.

That's it. For STBF, its four acts in volume one. This fiction only has one volume. And I want to finish it with a satisfactory ending. At least to myself, so I can have proof that I can finish the stories I start. It may take some days. But I'll do it and consider how STBF ended up. I'm sure this will be the first of my fictions to reach the finish line. We just have to see how long it takes.

As for Act 2. I believe I would be willing to take a bit of a break from this fiction. At this point it's just a tradition for me. Whenever I finish a substantial amount of content in a story/fiction I always take a break. Because breaks are surprisingly effective and can give a change of perspective.

I may be taking a minimum of 1 to 3 months break from this and Act 2 of STBF won't be happening until I push through my writer's block and finish EPMC Vol 3. It's already somewhere around 8.5k words deep. And I'm actively trying to make it better. Plus let's not forget SBM is the one that follows the continuous WN release schedule.

So I'll see you all beautiful readers in another segment of STBF and good bye for now~

- Rae ARK
