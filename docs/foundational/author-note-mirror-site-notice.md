Hello everyone,

It's me, Rae.

I recently discovered something I never expected to happen. All three of my web novels:

* Summoned by Mistake, I Decided to Learn How to Live
* Enigmatic Pathways: Mystic Circuits
* The Shadow I Cast Over Two Beautiful Flowers

have been copied and are being hosted on a website called Novels2.com without my permission.

To be honest, I'm still trying to process it.

I always knew that unauthorized aggregators existed, but I never imagined one would target my work as well. Finding my stories copied and republished elsewhere without my knowledge or consent has been incredibly disheartening.

I don't mind websites indexing my novels and linking readers to the original source. Databases and discovery sites can genuinely help readers find new stories. What I cannot accept is a website copying my work in its entirety and hosting it themselves. At that point, it stops being discovery and becomes unauthorized redistribution of my intellectual property.

So I'm asking for a small favor from anyone reading this.

Please don't read my work on sites like Novels2. If you'd like to support me, please read it through the official platforms where I've chosen to publish it. Examples include, Royal Road, Scribble Hub, Amazon KDP and the stores connected to it.

If you know other readers or writers, I'd also appreciate you spreading awareness about these kinds of mirror sites. Many authors, especially smaller ones, may not even realize their work has been copied elsewhere.

I'm also looking for advice.

If you've dealt with unauthorized mirror sites before, or you've gone through the copyright or DMCA process, I'd really appreciate hearing about your experience. Right now I'm trying to figure out the best way to respond and protect my work.

Thank you to everyone who's supported my writing over the years. It genuinely means a lot, and I hope we can continue building a community that respects the time and effort creators put into their work.

- Rae ARK
